For-Hire Bot — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 | Terms of Service

1. Data We Store

We only store Discord User IDs, chosen display names, skill tags, rates, turnaround times, pitches, portfolio links, credit balances, boost timers, and optional /partner Server ID + Whop usernames on secure, encrypted cloud servers to operate the bot.

2. What We Never Collect

We never read, log, or store server chat messages or private DMs, and we never sell user data or use it to train AI. All payments are processed securely by Whop.

3. Data Control & Deletion

Update your listing anytime via /profile, or request permanent deletion of your profile and data in our Discord Support Channel (https://discord.gg/sNfz8yCBDq) — processed within 24 hours.
