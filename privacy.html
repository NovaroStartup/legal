For-Hire Bot — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 | Terms of Service

1. Data We Store

  • We only store Discord User IDs, chosen display names, skill categories, rates, turnaround times, pitches, portfolio links, credit balances, boost timers, and Server IDs used for /claim-server perks.
  • All data is stored on secure, encrypted cloud servers strictly to operate the bot.

2. What We Never Collect

  • We never read, log, or store server chat messages or private DMs.
  • We never sell user data or use it to train AI.
  • All payments are processed securely by Whop—we never see or store card details.

3. Data Control & Deletion

  • Update or delete your listing anytime via /profile.
  • Request permanent deletion of all your data anytime in our Discord Support Channel (https://discord.gg/sNfz8yCBDq) — processed within 24 hours.
